Games Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

This policy covers every mobile game published by ogun.se ("the games", "we"), currently Bottle Smash and Crazy Cattle 3D. It explains what information the games handle, and why. In short: the games collect gameplay data to improve them (Bottle Smash only if you allow it). Their only ads are optional rewarded videos, and the ad partners that provide them collect some device information.

Who we are

The games are made by Ogün Gündogdu, an independent developer in Sweden, who is responsible for them and for this policy. You can reach him at support@ogun.se.

What the games store

Your progress and settings (such as sound and vibration) are saved only on your device. We do not run accounts, and the games do not send this information to us.

Gameplay analytics

Bottle Smash: the first time you open it, it asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay data. Only if you allow it, the game sends us events such as which level you completed or failed, together with basic device information (model, operating system, language and country), using Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies). We use this only to find levels that are too hard or too easy. You can turn it off any time in the game's Settings, which stops the game sending data.

Crazy Cattle 3D: while you play, the game sends us events such as rounds started and won, coins earned and spent, skins unlocked, and which rewarded videos were offered and watched, together with basic device information (model, operating system, language and country) and an identifier for your installation of the game. On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is only included if you allow tracking. It uses GameAnalytics and Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies). We use this to balance the game and to see how its rewarded videos perform. The game has no setting to turn this off; to stop it, delete the game, and contact us if you want the data already collected deleted. GameAnalytics describes its handling of this data in its privacy notice.

Ads

The games show only rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, for example to keep playing after losing a level. They are provided by Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), and in Crazy Cattle 3D also by Google AdMob (Google). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect:

  • device information, such as model, operating system, language and screen size;
  • your IP address and the approximate location it implies;
  • advertising and vendor identifiers. On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is only shared if you allow tracking when the game asks;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views, completions and taps.

Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and its FAQ. Google's is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Crazy Cattle 3D shows a consent message from Google the first time you open it, before any ads. There you can accept or refuse the use of your data for personalised ads and choose which partners may use it. You can change your choice any time with Privacy options in the game's main menu.

Your choices

  • Watching an ad is always optional; the games never show ads you did not ask for.
  • In Bottle Smash, gameplay analytics are off unless you allow them, and you can switch them off in Settings.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Crazy Cattle 3D asks for your consent to personalised ads, and Privacy options in its main menu lets you change it.
  • On iOS you can allow or deny tracking, and change it any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you deny it, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.
  • To ask Unity, Google or GameAnalytics for access to or deletion of data they hold about you, follow the steps in their privacy policies, or contact us and we will help.

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Sweden, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY).

Children

The games are for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when a new game uses a different ad partner, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
