Bottle Smash Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Bottle Smash mobile game ("the game", "we") handles, and why. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you. Its only ads are optional rewarded videos, and the ad partners that provide them collect some device information.

Who we are

Bottle Smash is made by Ogün Gündogdu, an independent developer in Sweden, who is responsible for the game and this policy. You can reach him at hello@ogun.se.

What the game stores

Your level progress and settings (sound, vibration) are saved only on your device. We do not run accounts, and the game does not send this information to us.

Ads

Bottle Smash shows only rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, for example to keep playing after losing a level. They are provided by Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect:

  • device information, such as model, operating system, language and screen size;
  • your IP address and the approximate location it implies;
  • advertising and vendor identifiers. On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is only shared if you allow tracking when the game asks;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views, completions and taps.

Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and its FAQ.

Your choices

  • Watching an ad is always optional; the game never shows ads you did not ask for.
  • On iOS you can allow or deny tracking, and change it any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you deny it, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.
  • To ask Unity for access to or deletion of data it holds about you, follow the steps in Unity's privacy policy, or contact us and we will help.

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Sweden, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY).

Children

Bottle Smash is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
