Test Bottle Smash

Bottle Smash is a colour-matching arcade game coming to Android. Help test it before release: it takes three taps, and you don't need to send anyone your email.

  1. Join the testers group

    Sign in with the Google account you use on your Android phone, then tap Join group. You won't get any emails from it.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    With the same Google account, tap Become a tester. If it says the test isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

    Become a tester

  3. Install it from Google Play

    Open this on your Android phone and install. Please keep it installed and play now and then for at least two weeks: Google needs that before the game can go public.

    Get it on Google Play

Android only for now. The test build shows real ads, but only when you choose to watch one. See the privacy policy. Questions or bugs: support@ogun.se
