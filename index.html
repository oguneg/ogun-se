Flagship

Sergeant Pace

An AI running coach that yells at you, plans your runs from couch to marathon, and is quietly terrified of you getting injured.

Knee hurts, stopped early. Effort 9.

Are you trying to retire early?! Effort 9 is way over your limit, and you only finished a quarter of the work. Stop running until that knee pain clears up and see a doctor if it persists.

log_runadjust_plan(step_back)

A real reply from the live app. The plan stepped back to shorter jogs and longer walk breaks, decided by the model but allowed by the code.

The model talks, the code decides

Gemini handles personality and judgment calls. Every number and every safety rule lives in plain, tested code. The model can't write a number into the plan.

Limits that slide, never jump

Effort and pace ceilings tighten smoothly with body fat and age. 29.7% and 30.1% get almost the same rules. Weigh-ins loosen or tighten them as you change.

Tested like production

124 tests, no API key needed. I broke the guardrails on purpose, ten different ways, to prove the tests catch it.

What broke once real people used it

  • Visitors killed each other's requests. Each conversation made its own Gemini client and overwrote a shared one; when two people arrived together the first was garbage collected mid-request. Found it within minutes of going live. Now one shared client, with a regression test.
  • New visitors waited 70 seconds. On a free-tier key the top models run out of daily quota, and every new conversation retried them all. Now it remembers the last model that worked. Second visitor: 9 seconds.
